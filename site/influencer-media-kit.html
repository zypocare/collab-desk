Questions

How often do the numbers update?

Every night, from Instagram’s official API.

Can brands see my audience details?

Yes, the age, gender and city breakdown Instagram shares (from 100 followers).

Do I need a professional Instagram account?

Yes, a Creator or Business account, which is free to switch to in Instagram settings.
