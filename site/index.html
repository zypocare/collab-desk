How a collab moves

From DM to paid, on one ticket.

01 · New requestA brand finds your link.

They send a proper request with the budget, dates and deliverables. Miracle scores it so you know at a glance if it's worth it.

02 · QuoteYour quote, in a tap.

Priced from your rate card: the fee, usage rights, revisions and advance, sent as a clean quote the brand can accept.

03 · DraftDrafts approved in the thread.

Send the cut, get notes, agree the go-live time. No lost WhatsApp voice notes.

04 · LiveIt goes live. Proof goes out.

Miracle spots the post and shares the numbers with the brand on a page they can open anytime.

05 · PaidAnd the money lands.

A GST invoice with a UPI QR for the exact amount, polite reminders, and a ping the moment it's paid.
