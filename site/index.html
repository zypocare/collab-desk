How a collab moves

From DM to paid, on one ticket.

01 · New requestA brand finds your link.

They send a proper request with the budget, dates and deliverables. Miracle scores it so you know at a glance if it's worth it.

02 · QuoteYour quote, in a tap.

Priced from your rate card: the fee, usage rights, revisions and advance, sent as a clean quote the brand can accept.

03 · DraftDrafts approved in the thread.

Send the cut, get notes, agree the go-live time. No lost WhatsApp voice notes.

Miracle spots the post and shares the numbers with the brand on a page they can open anytime.

For brands · How a campaign moves

One brief. Every creator.

01 · ShortlistPick creators who fit.

Search reviewed creators by niche, city and language, with their real Instagram numbers. The shortlist autopilot suggests more like them.

02 · BriefSend it to the whole pool.

Write the brief once: deliverables, dates and budget. Each creator gets their own request, and follow-ups go out for you.

03 · QuotesCompare quotes side by side.

Accept the ones within budget in a tap. Bigger quotes wait for an approver on your team.

04 · DraftsDrafts checked before you look.

Every caption is checked for your @mention, hashtags and #ad, so you only review what matters.

05 · Live & paidResults in. Invoices paid.

Posts are matched with their real reach. Pay each creator directly and keep the GST for your input credit.
