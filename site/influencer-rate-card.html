Questions

Good to know.

More answers in the help centre, or write to support@miracleapp.live.

How much should I charge for a reel?

It depends most on your average views, then engagement, niche and usage rights. The rate calculator and our guide walk through it.

Should I charge for usage rights?

Yes. When a brand runs your content as ads, it’s worth more to them; add 30% or more depending on how long.

How many quotes can I send for free?

3 a month on the free plan, and as many as you like on Creator Pro.

Can I show rates only to brands?

Yes: show them on your page, show “from” prices, or keep them for quotes.
