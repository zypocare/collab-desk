Questions

Good to know.

More answers in the help centre, or write to support@miracleapp.live.

Is Miracle free for brands?

There’s a free plan with no time limit: 3 requests and 1 campaign a month. Brand Pro (₹1,999 a month) removes the limits. There’s never a commission on deals.

Who handles GST and TDS?

Creators registered for GST send GST invoices you can claim; you deduct TDS as your accountant advises. Your plan’s own invoice from Miracle includes GST too.

Can creators apply to my brief?

Yes, on Brand Pro: open the brief, creators apply with their rate, and you shortlist.
