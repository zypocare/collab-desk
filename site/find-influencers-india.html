Questions

Good to know.

More answers in the help centre, or write to support@miracleapp.live.

Does Miracle charge brands a commission?

No. You pay creators directly and Miracle takes no cut.

What does the free plan include?

Discover with audience health, pools, briefs, drafts, approvals and reports, with 3 requests and 1 campaign a month. Brand Pro (₹1,999 a month, 14 days free) removes the limits and adds open briefs, brand autopilots, competitor watch, sales tracking and a team of 5. See pricing.

How do I know a creator’s followers are real?

Each connected creator has an audience health score from Instagram’s own data: reach, engagement for their size, sudden follower jumps and audience location.

Can an agency manage several brands?

Yes. On the Agency plan, each client is its own workspace and you switch between them, with no client seeing another’s data.
