Terms of service

The short version: run your collaborations here, be honest with each other, pay each other directly. Miracle never takes a cut.

1. What Miracle is

Miracle helps creators run brand collaborations (requests, quotes, drafts, invoices) and helps brands find creators and work with them. Miracle is run by Zyposoft Technologies Private Limited, Bengaluru. We provide the tool; the deal itself is between the creator and the brand.

2. Your account

You must be 18 or older, or have a parent or guardian agree to these terms for you. Give your real name and your real brand. Keep your email and phone safe: whoever signs in with them acts as you. Brands can add their team; each person signs in with their own email.

3. Deals and money

Fees, deliverables, dates and usage rights are agreed between the creator and the brand, and the agreement Miracle prepares records what you both accepted. Payments go straight from the brand to the creator by UPI or bank transfer. Miracle doesn't hold your money and takes no commission. Each of you is responsible for your own GST, TDS and income tax; invoices are made from the details you enter.

4. Your content

What you upload stays yours. You let us store it and show it to the people you share it with, and anyone can see a creator's public page. Usage rights for a post are what the creator and brand agreed in the request, not something Miracle grants.

5. Instagram

If you connect Instagram, we read your profile, insights and collaboration messages as Meta's rules allow, and only to run Miracle for you. We never post for you unless you schedule it. You can disconnect at any time in Settings or in Instagram.

6. What's not allowed

  • Pretending to be a brand or creator you're not.
  • Asking a creator to pay to get work, or for "registration" or "security" fees.
  • Harassment, threats, or sending requests nobody asked for in bulk.
  • Promoting anything illegal in India, or posts without the #ad or #collab label the ASCI guidelines ask for.
  • Copying creators' data out of Miracle with bots or scripts.

7. Reports, limits and suspension

Anyone can report a brand or creator, and block them. New accounts have daily limits on new requests and messages until they've done a collaboration or connected Instagram. If someone breaks these rules we may limit, pause or close their account. We tell you why, and you can reply to that email to appeal.

8. Leaving Miracle

Download your data or delete your account at any time in Settings. Your account closes at once and is erased 30 days later; sign in before then to undo it. Invoices are kept only as long as Indian tax law requires.

9. Price and changes

Miracle has a free plan and paid plans (Creator Pro, Brand Pro and Agency), monthly or yearly, at the prices on our pricing page, including GST. Paid plans are bought on our website or web app through Razorpay and renew by themselves until you stop renewal; they then run to the end of the period paid for. New accounts get a 14-day trial of Pro. Everyone using Miracle before paid plans started keeps Pro free until 31 March 2027, and we'll email you before that ends. Refunds follow our refund and cancellation policy. Paid plans never add a commission on deals: brands still pay creators directly. If we change a price, we email you at least 30 days before it applies to you. When these terms change in a way that matters, we email you before it takes effect.

10. Liability

We work hard to keep Miracle running and your data safe, but it's offered as it is. We're not responsible for what a brand or creator does or doesn't do in a deal. Our total liability to you is limited to what you paid Miracle in the 12 months before the claim.

11. Law and disputes

These terms follow the laws of India. Disputes go to the courts in Bengaluru.

12. Grievance officer

Under India's IT Rules, complaints about content or accounts go to our grievance officer. We reply within 24 hours and resolve it within 15 days.
