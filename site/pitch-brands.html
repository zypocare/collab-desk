What you get

Pitching, on autopilot.

01

Finds brands that fit

Every morning it searches for brands in your niche (and near your city) that work with creators, up to 10 new ones a day.

02

Only real inboxes

It checks each brand’s own website for a public business email. Brands without one are left out, so pitches don’t bounce.

03

A pitch in your voice

Each pitch is written for that brand: why you fit, a content idea, and your link. You can edit any of them.

04

Approve or auto

In approve mode each pitch waits for your tap. In auto they go out on their own, one at a time between 10 am and 6 pm.

05

Follow-ups, once

No reply? A polite follow-up goes out later. A brand is never pitched twice.

06

Replies become deals

When a brand answers, it becomes a request on your desk, with a quote you can send in a minute.

How it works

Up and running in a day.

  1. Set your niche, city and packages.
  2. Open Settings › Auto-pilot and switch on brand pitches.
  3. Choose approve or auto, and how many brands a day.
  4. Check your pitches each morning, or let them run.
Questions

Good to know.

More answers in the help centre, or write to support@miracleapp.live.

Is the pitch autopilot free?

It’s part of Creator Pro (₹299 a month, with 14 days free to try). On the free plan you can still write and send 5 pitches a month yourself.

Where does the brand email come from?

From the brand’s own website: Miracle only uses public business emails it finds there.

Can I see the pitch before it goes?

Yes. In approve mode nothing goes out until you tap send, and you can edit each pitch.

Will it spam brands?

No. It sends a few a day, one at a time, follows up once, and never pitches the same brand twice.
