Still stuck?
In the app, open Help › Contact support, or email support@miracleapp.live.
  1. Install the Miracle app (Android from the Play Store; on iPhone, add the site to your Home Screen until the App Store app is out).
  2. Sign in and allow notifications when asked.
  3. In Settings › Inbox, replies & alerts, choose what you want to hear about, and tap Send a test.

Every important alert is also kept in Alerts (the bell on Today) for 90 days, so nothing is lost if you miss a notification. You can delete alerts there, one by one or all at once.

Not getting alerts? Check your phone’s battery saver isn’t blocking Miracle, and that notifications are on for it in your phone’s settings.
