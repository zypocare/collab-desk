Plans, the free trial and billing

Miracle is free for creators; what Brand Pro and Agency add, the brand trial, and how to pay or stop renewing.

Miracle is free for creators, with no time limit: unlimited collabs, offer checks and pitches, every autopilot, reminders that go by themselves, bank-credit matching, the formal demand letter and MSME complaint, your insights page and fan list, and one teammate. Invoices and your page carry a small “Sent with Miracle” line. Brands on Free open 10 full creator profiles a day and send 3 requests and 1 campaign a month.

Brand Pro (₹1,999 a month) removes the brand limits and adds open briefs, brand autopilots, sales tracking and a team of 5. Agency (₹4,999 a month) covers every creator you manage and your own brand team. A yearly plan is about two months free. The plans on sale are always listed in Plan & billing.

A collab counts from the first quote you send until it’s wrapped, declined, cancelled or paused. Creators a brand has worked with never count towards its 10 profiles a day.

New brand teams start with 14 days of Brand Pro, no card needed. When it ends they stay on the free plan unless they pick one; nothing they made is deleted.

  1. Open Settings › Plan & billing.
  2. Pick a plan, monthly or yearly.
  3. Pay on the web with UPI, a card or net banking (through Razorpay).
  4. You get a receipt and an invoice by email, and the plan is on everywhere, the phone app too.

Plans renew by themselves. Stop renewing in Plan & billing at any time: the plan stays on until the end of what you paid for. A first payment can be refunded within 7 days.

Your invite link is in Plan & billing: share it with creator friends. Brands also see it on your page, invoices and quotes.

There’s never a commission on deals, on any plan. Brands pay creators directly.
