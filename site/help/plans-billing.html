Plans, the free trial and billing

What’s free, what Pro adds, how the 14-day trial works, and how to pay or stop renewing.

Miracle has a free plan with no time limit. Creators get 3 quotes, 3 invoices and 5 pitches a month; brands get 3 requests and 1 campaign a month. Pro removes the limits and turns on the autopilots: Creator Pro is ₹299 a month, Brand Pro ₹1,999 a month, and Agency ₹4,999 a month. A yearly plan is about two months free.

Everyone starts with 14 days of Pro, no card needed. When it ends you stay on the free plan unless you pick one; nothing you made is deleted.

  1. Open Settings › Plan & billing.
  2. Pick a plan, monthly or yearly.
  3. Pay on the web with UPI, a card or net banking (through Razorpay).
  4. You get a receipt and an invoice by email, and the plan is on everywhere, the phone app too.

Plans renew by themselves. Stop renewing in Plan & billing at any time: the plan stays on until the end of what you paid for. A first payment can be refunded within 7 days.

There’s never a commission on deals, on any plan. Brands pay creators directly.
