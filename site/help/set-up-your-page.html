Set up your page and link

Your own address (name.miracleapp.live) where brands see your rates and send requests.

Every creator on Miracle gets a page at yourname.miracleapp.live. Brands open it to see who you are, your live Instagram numbers, your packages and rates, and to send you a request in a minute.

  1. Open Settings › Profile & Instagram and fill in your name, niche, city and the line about you.
  2. Connect Instagram so your followers, views and audience show live (and stay up to date every night).
  3. Add packages in Settings › Rates & packages, so brands can book you directly.
  4. Copy your link and put it in your Instagram bio.

Settings › Get found by brands shows who opened your page and from where, and can write your “about” line with AI.
