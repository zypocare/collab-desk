In the app, open Help › Contact support, or email support@miracleapp.live.

In Settings › Team, add someone by email and choose their role. They sign in with their own email and work on your desk.

  • Manager: handles requests, quotes, collabs and messages.
  • Assistant: helps with collabs and content, without the money side.
  • Accountant: invoices, payments and tax.

You can change a role or remove someone at any time. Each person has their own alerts and their own Alerts list.
