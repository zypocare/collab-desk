In the app, open Help › Contact support, or email support@miracleapp.live.

Tap Book again on a live or finished request, or next to the creator in Collabs › Creators. The form is filled in from that collab. Creators can also send you a Book again link by email or WhatsApp.

If the creator offers a returning-brand discount, you’ll see Welcome back and the % off, taken off their quote while the offer lasts.

The Rebook autopilot offers your best creators (by cost per 1,000 views) the same brief again when a collab ends.
