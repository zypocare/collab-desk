How brand requests reach you

From your page, email and Instagram DMs, all in one place.

Brands can send a request from your page (the quickest way, with the brief, budget and dates). When Instagram is connected, Miracle also reads brand DMs and flags the ones that look like a collab, so you can turn them into a request.

New requests show on Today and in Collabs. Miracle’s AI reads each one and tells you how well it fits, whether the budget is fair for you, and what to ask.

Away for a while? Turn on Away mode in Settings › Bookings & barter, and brands are told when you’re back.
