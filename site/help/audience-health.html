Every night Miracle scores an Instagram-connected creator’s audience from their own numbers: real reach (how many followers a post reaches), engagement for an account that size, sudden follower jumps no viral post explains, and how much of the audience is in India.

Once a week an AI also reads the last 40 captions for things a brand should know first, such as betting apps, adult content, risky health claims, or promotions without #ad. Minor notes (like drinks at a night out) don’t count against anyone; only serious ones mark a creator as worth a look.

  • To lift reach and engagement: post consistently, reply to comments, and make reels people share and save.
  • A flag you think is wrong: open the post, edit the caption or archive it, and it clears at the next check. Or raise a ticket and we’ll look.
