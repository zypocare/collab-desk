Send a request to a creator

From their page or Discover: brief, budget, dates, done.

  1. Open the creator’s page (name.miracleapp.live) or find them in Find creators › Discover.
  2. Tap Send a request: what you want made, your budget (or ask for a quote), dates and the brief.
  3. Sign in with your work email (a 6-digit code, no password). Track everything in Requests.

The creator replies or sends a quote you can accept online. Drafts, messages, invoices and results all stay on the request.
