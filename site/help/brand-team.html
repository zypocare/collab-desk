In Settings, invite colleagues with their work email and a role: admin, approver (accepts quotes and drafts), finance (invoices and payments) or member. Agencies can add several companies and switch between them.

Working for several clients (an agency, say)? Each client invites you to its own team. Joining one never shows it another client’s companies, requests or money. A Working for button appears in the menu: tap it to see every client side by side (open requests, what’s waiting on you, approvals, money to pay) and to switch. You see one client at a time.
