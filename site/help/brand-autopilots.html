Brand autopilots and turnaround

Shortlists, follow-ups, draft checks, counters and rebooking on approve or auto, and a clock on every step.

In Autopilots, each one is off, ask (it prepares the step and waits for your approval) or auto (it goes ahead and tells you).

  • Shortlist: finds creators who fit each campaign (niche, content, engagement, price, past work with you).
  • Follow-up: a friendly nudge when a creator hasn’t quoted, a draft is late or a post is due.
  • Draft checker: checks each caption against your brief (mentions, hashtags, promo code, link, #ad).
  • Negotiator: accepts quotes within your budget, and asks for a lower price on ones a little above it.
  • Rebook: offers your best creators the same brief again when a collab ends.
  • Competitor watch: spots creators who work with up to five competitors and invites them to a campaign you pick.

Turnaround (in Business › Turnaround) gives every step an owner and a target: replying to a quote, approving a draft, paying an invoice. When a step runs late, the owner is reminded, and then the next person up.

Brand autopilots, competitor watch, open briefs, sales tracking and turnaround are part of Brand Pro, and on during your 14-day trial.
