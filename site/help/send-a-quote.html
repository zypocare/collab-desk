Send a quote and get it accepted

Quote with options, usage rights and dates; the brand accepts online.

Still stuck?
In the app, open Help › Contact support, or email support@miracleapp.live.
  1. Open the request in Collabs and tap Build quote.
  2. Add what you’ll make, your fee, usage rights and dates. You can offer up to three options (for example a reel, or a reel with stories).
  3. Send it. The brand gets an email and accepts online with their name.

When they accept, Miracle makes a simple agreement both sides can download. If they ask for changes, you’ll see their note and can send a new version.

Got a contract or offer from the brand? Use Check the offer to have AI read it against your rates and usual terms, and write your reply.
