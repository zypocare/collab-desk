Connect Instagram (and reconnect it)

Why Miracle asks, what it reads, and what to do if numbers stop updating.

Connecting Instagram lets Miracle show your real numbers to brands (followers, reach, views, engagement and audience), spot when a collab post goes live, and keep brand DMs together in your Inbox.

  • You need an Instagram professional account (Creator or Business). Switch in Instagram › Settings › Account type and tools.
  • Miracle reads your own posts, insights and audience. It never posts without you, and never reads personal chats that aren’t about collabs.
  • Numbers refresh every night. Audience details (age, city, country) need 100+ followers.

If numbers stop updating, Instagram may have signed Miracle out (it does this every 60 days, or when you change your password). Open Settings › Profile & Instagram and tap Connect again.
