Connecting Instagram lets Miracle show your real numbers to brands (followers, reach, views, engagement and audience), notice when a collab post goes live, make results reports, and spot brand DMs that look like a collab.

  • You need an Instagram professional account (Creator or Business). Switch in Instagram › Settings › Account type and tools.
  • Connect in My page › Profile & Instagram (Settings on a laptop). Miracle reads your own posts, insights and audience. It never posts without you.
  • DMs and posting from the app need one more permission: tap Allow DMs and posting on the same screen. Only DMs that look like collabs are kept.
  • Numbers sync every morning at 6. Tap Sync now for a fresh read. Audience details (age, gender, city, country) are read weekly and need 100+ followers.

Miracle keeps the connection fresh by itself. If it says Last sync failed, Instagram has signed Miracle out (usually after a password change). Tap Reconnect on the same screen.

The same screen has Automatic go-live and Results reports for brands. See “How Miracle knows your post is live”.
