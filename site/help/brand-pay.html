Paying creators

You pay the creator directly; Miracle takes no commission.

Still stuck?
In the app, open Help › Contact support, or email support@miracleapp.live.

The creator sends an invoice (with GST if they’re registered) on the request. Pay them directly by UPI or bank transfer using the details on it, then open the invoice and tap I’ve paid this invoice (with the UTR) so they can confirm. Deduct TDS as your accountant advises.

Miracle never handles the money and takes no commission. Your payment record (how quickly you pay) shows to creators, so paying on time helps you book the best ones.
