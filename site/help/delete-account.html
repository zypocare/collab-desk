Download or delete your data

Take a copy of everything, or close your account.

Creators: Settings › Sign-in, devices & account lets you download all your data or delete your account. Brands can do the same in Settings › Account. Deleting removes your data from Miracle, except what the law asks us to keep (such as invoices).

See the privacy policy for what Miracle keeps and why.
