How Miracle knows your post is live

Automatic go-live detection and the #ad check.

With Instagram connected, Miracle notices when the collab post goes up (it looks for the brand’s tag or name), marks the collab as live, and checks the post is labelled as an ad, as ASCI rules ask.

Seven days later it makes a results report (views, reach, likes, saves, shares) you can send to the brand in one tap.

Posted but not detected? Open the collab and link the post yourself.

Still stuck? In the app, open Help › Contact support, or email support@miracleapp.live.
