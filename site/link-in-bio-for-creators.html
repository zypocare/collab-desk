How to start.

  1. Join free and choose your address (yourname.miracleapp.live).

  4. Paste the link in your Instagram bio.

Questions

Is it a replacement for Linktree?

It does what a link list does (links for followers, clicks counted) and adds what brands need: packages, prices, stats and a request form.

Can I hide my prices?

Yes. Show prices, show “from” prices, or let brands ask for a quote.

Can I use my own domain?

Your page lives at yourname.miracleapp.live, which you can share anywhere.
