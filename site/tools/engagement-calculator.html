By followers

The number most brands ask for

Likes, comments, saves and shares a post, divided by followers, as a percentage. Easy to check for any public account, so it's what brands compare.

By views

The truer one for reels

Reels reach people who don't follow you, so dividing by views says how much the people who saw it cared. Brands that buy reels look at this more and more.

Saves and shares

What brands value most

A save or share means someone wants to come back or tell a friend. If you can, include them: they show the post worked, not just that it was seen.
