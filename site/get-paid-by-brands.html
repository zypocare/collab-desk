Questions

Good to know.

More answers in the help centre, or write to support@miracleapp.live.

Does Miracle collect the payment?

No. Brands pay you directly; Miracle only tracks it and reminds them.

Do I need GST to send an invoice?

No. Most creators aren’t registered for GST, and Miracle makes a normal invoice with your UPI QR. If you are registered, switch it on and GST is added for you.

What if a brand never pays?

Miracle helps you escalate step by step, with a formal demand letter and, for MSME-registered creators, an MSME complaint.

Can I see if a brand pays late before I agree?

Yes, when they’ve worked with creators on Miracle, you see how quickly they usually pay.
