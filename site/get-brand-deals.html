Questions

Good to know.

More answers in the help centre, or write to support@miracleapp.live.

How many followers do I need to get brand deals?

There’s no minimum. Brands book nano and micro creators (from a few thousand followers) for their trust and engagement. What matters most is a clear niche, steady reach and a page that makes booking easy.

Does Miracle take a cut of my deals?

No. Brands pay you directly by UPI or bank transfer and Miracle takes no commission, on any plan.

What’s free and what’s Pro?

Your page, the directory listing, requests, offer checks and the collab board are free, with 3 quotes, 3 invoices and 5 pitches a month. Creator Pro (₹299 a month, 14 days free) adds the autopilots, unlimited quotes and invoices, results reports and your team. See pricing.

Will the AI send pitches without asking me?

Only if you choose auto. By default each pitch waits for you to approve it, and a brand is never pitched twice.

Can I use Miracle if brands already DM me?

Yes. Miracle reads brand DMs on connected Instagram accounts, flags the ones that look like a collab, and turns them into requests you can quote on.
