How to start.

  1. Bring your current collabs in (or start fresh).
  2. Answer new requests with a quote.
  3. Share drafts for approval.
  4. Send the results and the invoice when it’s live.

Questions

Can my manager use it?

Yes. Add a manager, assistant or accountant; each signs in with their own email and sees what their role needs.

Do brands need an account?

They sign in with their work email (a 6-digit code, no password) to follow the request, approve drafts and see invoices.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, there’s an Android app and it works in any browser.
