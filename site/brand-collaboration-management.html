Questions

Good to know.

More answers in the help centre, or write to support@miracleapp.live.

Can my manager use it?

Yes, on Creator Pro. Add a manager, assistant or accountant; each signs in with their own email and sees what their role needs. Managers looking after several creators use the Agency plan.

Do brands need an account?

They sign in with their work email (a 6-digit code, no password) to follow the request, approve drafts and see invoices.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Miracle works in any browser on phone and laptop, and the Android app is coming to the Play Store.
