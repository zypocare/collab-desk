Questions

Good to know.

Anything else? Write to support@miracleapp.live.

Is there a commission on deals?

No, never. Brands pay creators directly by UPI or bank transfer, and Miracle takes nothing from any deal on any plan. Plans pay for the tools, not for the deals.

How does the free trial work?

Every new creator and brand gets 14 days of Pro, with no card needed. When it ends you stay on the free plan unless you pick a paid one; nothing you made is deleted.

How do I pay?

On the website or the web app, through Razorpay: UPI AutoPay, cards or net banking. Plans renew by themselves and you can stop renewal any time. Plans aren’t sold inside the Play Store or iPhone apps; once you’ve paid, they unlock there too.

Is GST included?

Yes. Prices include 18% GST, and every payment comes with a tax invoice from Zyposoft Technologies Private Limited, by email and as a link.

What is the founding member price?

The first 100 people to buy a yearly plan pay half, and keep that price for as long as their plan keeps renewing.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 7 days of your first payment for a plan, if it isn’t for you. See the refund and cancellation policy.

I manage several creators or clients. Which plan?

The Agency plan: Pro for every creator you manage and for your own brand team, client workspaces you switch between, a roster with your commission ledger, and 15 seats.
