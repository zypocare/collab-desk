Questions

Good to know.

More answers in the help centre, or write to support@miracleapp.live.

How much is the Agency plan?

₹4,999 a month, or ₹49,999 a year (founding members pay ₹24,999 a year). It includes Pro for every creator you manage, your own brand team and 15 seats. See pricing.

Does Miracle take a share of our commission?

No. Brands pay creators directly, you settle your commission with your creators as you do today, and Miracle only keeps the ledger.

Can a creator leave my roster?

Yes. A creator can remove a manager in their settings at any time; their own data stays with them.

Can clients see each other?

No. Each client workspace is separate, and switching shows one client at a time.
